# Preliminary Terms Acknowledgement

**Purpose.** This document records the principal terms on which the prospective service provider (the **Provider**) is willing to enter into an initial meeting and further discussions with the prospective client (the **Client**). The Client is requested to review and acknowledge these terms before the meeting proceeds. This acknowledgement does not itself constitute an order for services, authorise work to commence, or replace a subsequently accepted proposal or service agreement.

## Principal terms

1. **Nature of the services and absence of guaranteed results.** The Provider will perform the services and deliverables expressly agreed in writing with reasonable care and skill. Unless expressly guaranteed in a later written agreement, the Provider does not guarantee enquiries, leads, appointments, sales, revenue, profit, growth, return on investment, search rankings, social media reach, or any other commercial result. Illustrations, demonstrations, forecasts, and estimates are not contractual commitments unless expressly included in the agreed scope.

2. **Client business responsibilities.** The Client remains responsible for its products and services, pricing, business claims, customer relationships, sales follow-up, negotiations, fulfilment, and commercial decisions. The Provider will undertake any such activity only where expressly included in the agreed scope.

3. **Defined scope and changes.** The parties will record the applicable services, deliverables, quantities, timelines, fees, review process, and acceptance criteria in writing before work commences. Additional features, content, integrations, campaigns, revisions, or other requirements outside the agreed scope will require written approval of any additional fees and revised timelines before chargeable work is undertaken.

4. **Client inputs and delivery schedule.** The Client will provide accurate information, materials, necessary permissions, access, and timely consolidated approvals through an authorised representative. Where a required input, access, or approval is delayed, the planned delivery date will be extended by the number of working days lost to that delay. Resuming work may require further reasonable time according to resource availability. The Provider will communicate any material impact and the revised schedule.

5. **AI and third-party dependencies.** AI-generated material may contain errors or require review. External platforms, including AI providers, search engines, social networks, advertising platforms, and hosting services, control their own availability, policies, approvals, and algorithms. The Provider remains responsible for its agreed work but cannot guarantee third-party decisions or uninterrupted operation.

6. **AI tool, token, and other external costs.** The Client will bear the cost of AI tool subscriptions, model token usage, and other AI usage required for the agreed services. Before chargeable use, the parties will record the applicable tools, anticipated charges or usage limits, billing arrangements, and authorisation process in writing. Other external costs, including advertising spend, hosting, domains, licences, messaging, and subscriptions, will likewise be identified and agreed before they are incurred on the Client's behalf. The Provider will not incur additional chargeable usage on the Client's behalf without the agreed authorisation.

7. **Purpose of setup and recurring service fees.** The setup fee reflects the work required to understand the Client's business, develop and refine prompts for its particular use case, and design, configure, and test the agreed automations. The monthly recurring fee covers the ongoing activities specified in the service agreement, which may include monitoring, reviewing performance, and refining prompts and workflows as AI models, platform behaviour, and business requirements change. Such ongoing improvement is a commitment to perform the agreed activities, not a guarantee of a particular result. AI tool and token charges are separate from the Provider's service fees.

8. **Payment, commencement, and cancellation.** The later written agreement will specify the setup fee, recurring fee, payment schedule, commencement trigger, and cancellation terms. Once substantive work on an agreed service has commenced, amounts paid toward that commenced work, including an applicable setup fee or advance, are non-refundable, subject to applicable law and any remedies arising from the Provider's failure to fulfil its agreed obligations. Payment alone does not constitute commencement. The treatment of unused third-party funds and prepaid future service periods will be stated separately in the agreement.

9. **Delivery and acceptance.** Delivery and acceptance will be assessed against the agreed scope and acceptance criteria, rather than anticipated commercial results. Material defects in the agreed work will be addressed under the acceptance or warranty process specified in the later agreement.
