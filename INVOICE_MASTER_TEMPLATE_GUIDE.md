# Master Invoice Content Guide

A reusable source for preparing project, website, software, automation, digital marketing, and ongoing service invoices. It captures the scope pointers and contract clause structure from the Bharat AI Vyapari invoice supplied as a reference, while replacing client-specific names, prices, dates, and details with fields to confirm.

This file is a drafting aid. It is not a signed agreement or legal advice. Before issuing an invoice, confirm that each commercial and legal term matches the signed commitment and applicable law. Remove clauses that do not apply; do not leave placeholders in a final invoice.

## 1. Invoice structure

Use the applicable parts of this structure:

1. **Invoice title and status** — invoice number, issue date, due date, payment status, and a short billing descriptor (for example, project milestone or recurring retainer).
2. **Billed By / Billed To** — verified legal names, billing addresses, tax identifiers, and approved contact details.
3. **Item table** — item/service name, description, quantity, unit rate, taxable value, tax rate and amount, total, and currency.
4. **Scope and deliverables** — describe the agreed work under each line item. Keep one-time project work distinct from recurring services.
5. **Totals and payment details** — subtotal, taxes, total in words if required, payments received, balance due, payment instructions, and authorised signatory.
6. **Terms and Conditions as mentioned in signed commitment** — use this title only when the invoice reflects the signed commitment. Otherwise use a clearly labelled proposed-terms heading until the terms are agreed.
7. **Revisions Policy**
8. **Project Completion & Closure**
9. **No Refund Policy**
10. **No Guarantee of Results**
11. **General Closure Provisions**
12. **Client notes and payment schedule**, where relevant.

## 4. Terms and conditions

Use only clauses supported by the agreement. Replace bracketed fields with confirmed values and align this section with the scope and payment schedule.

1. **Billing:** [Billing cadence, invoice timing, due date, tax treatment, and payment method]. For recurring work, state the service commencement trigger and whether billing is in advance or arrears.
2. **Retainer Terms (if applicable):** The **[periodic]** retainer of **[amount]** is a recurring service fee billed **[in advance / in arrears]** from **[start trigger]**. It continues **[term / month-to-month]** and covers **[services, usage limits, and exclusions]**. State renewal and cancellation terms.
3. **Deemed Approval (if agreed):** A deliverable submitted for review is deemed approved if the client does not provide written feedback within **[number] business days**. State the resulting next step and whether the relevant stage closes.
4. **Approval is Final (if agreed):** Once a stage is approved expressly or under the agreed deemed-approval process, it is locked. Later changes to that stage, including changes arising from updated preferences, management, branding, or strategy, are handled as a separately quoted change request.
5. **Single Point of Approval (if agreed):** The client will nominate one authorised person to consolidate and submit feedback and approvals. Conflicting or fragmented feedback is held until consolidated.
6. **Operational Costs:** Identify what is included and excluded, including hosting, domains, AI/API tokens, image-generation credits, SEO/analytics tools, messaging, plugins, licences, and other third-party services. State who owns/funds accounts, usage limits, approval requirements for new costs, consequences of depleted credits or unpaid accounts, and renewal costs after any included period.

## 5. Revisions Policy

1. **Included Revisions:** Include **[number]** revision rounds per **[screen / deliverable / stage]** at **[stage]**, and **[number]** correction round(s) during **[UAT / final review stage]**. State what deliverables are covered.
2. **Definition of a Revision:** A revision is a refinement within the approved direction and scope, such as changes to colour, spacing, copy, imagery, or minor layout. One round means one consolidated feedback set submitted in **[format/channel]** and actioned in one pass.
3. **Billable Change Requests:** Separately quote changes in design direction or visual concept after approval; new pages, screens, templates, modules, or features; rebranding or logo changes; changes to architecture or navigation; changes to already-developed/approved work; and feedback submitted after a stage closes. Add any project-specific items.
4. **Feedback Window:** Revision feedback must be submitted within **[number] business days** after delivery. State whether unused rounds expire and whether they can be transferred or refunded.

## 6. Project Completion & Closure

1. **Definition of Completion:** The project is considered complete when the modules and deliverables listed in the invoice are deployed and functional on **[live domain / agreed environment]**, subject to the agreed acceptance criteria. Commercial performance, marketing outcomes, and later subjective preferences do not by themselves change the delivery scope.
2. **Final Acceptance:** After go-live or final delivery, the client has **[number] calendar days** for final UAT and must report reproducible defects through **[channel]**. At the end of that period, **[state the agreed acceptance and closure effect]**.
3. **Closure:** Once accepted or closed under the agreed process, the engagement is concluded. Additional design, redesign, restructuring, migration, re-platforming, or development requires a new scope and quotation unless expressly included in the warranty or support terms.
4. **No Rework Obligation:** Post-closure requests to redesign, restyle, restructure, migrate, or re-platform are treated as new work and charged at **[agreed rates / separately quoted rates]**, except for covered defects during a valid warranty period.
5. **Handover:** On **[full payment / other agreed condition]**, the client receives the agreed source code, administrative credentials, hosting/domain access, and documentation. List exceptions, third-party materials, and items retained by the provider. State the effect of handover on delivery obligations.
6. **Limited Defect Warranty (if agreed):** For **[number] days** after go-live, functional defects in the delivered modules that fail to meet the agreed scope will be corrected without additional charge. Exclude changes in design/content, new requirements, client-side edits, third-party plugin/API/platform changes, hosting/server issues, and items outside the listed scope, as applicable.

## 7. No Refund Policy

Use wording consistent with applicable law and the signed agreement; adjust for any mandatory consumer rights, express refund commitments, or service failures.

1. **Payments:** State which payments are non-refundable and from what point, such as after receipt, commencement, allocation of work, or delivery of a milestone. Identify development charges, milestone payments, advances, and recurring fees separately where they have different treatment.
2. **Refund exclusions:** If agreed, specify how cancellation/abandonment, changes in client plans or ownership, dissatisfaction with subjective preferences after approval, commercial outcomes, or client-caused delays affect refunds.
3. **Transferability:** State whether amounts paid can be transferred to another project, entity, or service and what written approval is required.
4. **Mid-project termination:** State how completed and in-progress work is valued, what remains payable, and how prior payments are applied or treated.

## 8. No Guarantee of Results

1. **No Business Results Guarantee:** The provider makes no representation or guarantee regarding sales, revenue, enquiries, RFQs, leads, conversions, profitability, or business growth from the delivered work. Outcomes depend on factors including pricing, product, market conditions, competition, sales execution, and other factors outside the provider's control.
2. **No SEO Ranking Guarantee:** Search rankings, traffic, impressions, and indexation are controlled by search engines. The provider commits to the agreed deliverables and SEO practices, not specific positions, traffic figures, or timelines. Algorithm updates, competitor activity, and search-engine actions are outside the provider's control.
3. **No AI Content Guarantee:** AI-generated or automated content is produced on a best-effort basis. State the client's responsibility to review and approve published content for factual accuracy, technical correctness, and legal compliance.
4. **No Third-Party Guarantee:** Define responsibility for outages, deprecations, price or policy changes, or discontinuation of third-party services, hosting providers, APIs, plugins, and platforms.
5. **Statements Not Warranties:** Projections, estimates, examples, and opinions are illustrative unless expressly incorporated as a written contractual commitment.

## 9. General Closure Provisions

1. **Entire Agreement:** Identify the documents that make up the agreement and explain how they relate. State that earlier verbal discussions, chats, proposals, or drafts are superseded only to the extent permitted by the signed agreement and applicable law.
2. **Scope Freeze:** The scope is limited to the deliverables expressly listed in the invoice and incorporated agreement. Unlisted requirements are excluded even if discussed, unless added through the agreed written change process.
3. **Client Delay:** If the client fails to provide required inputs or approvals for **[number] consecutive days**, state the provider's rights to pause, reschedule, close as delivered, and/or invoice outstanding amounts. Define notice and restart conditions.
4. **Liability Cap:** State the agreed aggregate liability cap and excluded categories of loss, subject to applicable law and any exceptions in the signed agreement.
5. **Indemnity:** Define the client's responsibility for claims arising from client-supplied or client-approved product data, technical specifications, brand assets, and content, including intellectual property or misdescription claims, subject to agreed limits and applicable law.
6. **Acceptance:** State how acceptance occurs (for example, signature, written approval, or payment) and ensure this matches the agreement and applicable law.

## 10. Client notes and payment schedule

Keep operational instructions separate from legal terms. Include only confirmed actions, owners, channels, and dates.

- **Client inputs:** [Catalogue, assets, data, access, and due dates].
- **Approvals:** [Authorised approver, review window, submission channel].
- **Testing:** [UAT period, test responsibilities, defect-reporting channel].
- **Usage and accounts:** [Who funds and monitors third-party or AI usage].
- **Payment Schedule:** For each milestone, state its name, amount and tax basis, due date or trigger, purpose, and verified payment status. Keep amounts consistent with invoice totals and distinguish received payments from future scheduled payments.

## 11. Invoice preparation checklist

- Confirm client identity, seller details, invoice date, due date, currency, tax treatment, prices, and payments from current records.
- Distinguish one-time project fees from recurring retainer charges.
- Tie every scope pointer to an agreed deliverable; state quantity, limits, dependencies, and exclusions.
- Confirm revision counts, feedback windows, acceptance process, closure timing, handover conditions, and any defect warranty.
- Confirm refund, liability, indemnity, and result-disclaimer wording against the signed commitment and applicable law.
- Remove inapplicable clauses and resolve every placeholder before issuing.
- Ensure the payment schedule, tax calculations, received payments, and balance due reconcile.
- Never copy historical client names, amounts, invoice dates, payment records, or contact details into a new invoice.
